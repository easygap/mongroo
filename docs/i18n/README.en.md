# Mongroo

**Keep a journal, raise a character, and take them on adventures.**

In Mongroo, writing about your day helps your character grow. Build a party with the companions you've raised, collect new characters, and decorate a room of your own.

Mongroo is being prepared for release and isn't available in app stores yet. The game interface and dialogue are currently in Korean. You can read this introduction in any of the four languages above.

## Start with a few lines about your day

Write about whatever you want to remember. Saving an entry earns experience and helps your character grow. Writing about sadness or anger won't cost you experience or rewards.

Tap a date on the calendar to reread or edit an entry. Weekly and monthly summaries let you look back at how you've been feeling.

## Raise and collect 18 characters

Each character grows through five stages, starting as a seed. The emotions in your journal shape one of six forms, changing how your character looks and speaks. No emotion makes them grow faster or earns better rewards.

Once a character is fully grown, add them to your museum and plant a new seed. Bring the characters you've collected on adventures and try out their different skills.

## Complete quests and choose what to unlock

Daily quests reward you with experience and **Seeds**, the in-game currency. Save up for new characters, outfits, and room decorations. You can see each item's price and requirements before buying it. There are no gacha pulls.

## Take your companions exploring

Explore 32 stages across four regions. Pick a destination on the map, move your character through the area, and discover stories along the way.

In battle, check the enemy's next move before choosing an attack, a defensive move, or a skill. Press and hold a skill to read more about it. Auto-battle, battle speed, music, sound effects, and reduced motion are all available in settings.

## Decorate your room

Choose a room theme, arrange decorations, and dress up your characters. Use the Seeds you earn as you play to pick up the pieces you like.

## Try it before creating an account

After launching the app, choose **회원가입 없이 3분 체험** (“3-minute trial without signing up”) on the login screen to try journaling and character growth. Trial entries stay on your device and aren't sent to the server. They won't transfer automatically if you create an account later.
